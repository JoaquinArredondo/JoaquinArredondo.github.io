Romance in Video Games · Game AI Research

Emotion-driven Utility AI for NPC response selection

A Unity research prototype exploring a more systemic alternative to single affinity meters and fixed romance progression.

Problem

Transactional romance mechanics often collapse relationship dynamics into one score or fixed dialogue thresholds.

Solution

We separated player intent, continuous emotional state, relationship dimensions, personality bias, and Utility AI response scoring.

Implementation

Unity/C#, 46 actions → 9 intents, PAD state/deltas, Utility AI considerations and curves, ScriptableObjects, personality multipliers.

Implementation truth: Action-to-intent mapping, PAD updates, and emotional utility scoring are implemented in the current prototype. Relationship runtime wiring is incomplete, personality content needs migration, and response execution is mostly a semantic/debug stub rather than finished dialogue/animation.

Path of Anu · Unity Developer / Technical Game Designer

Spell-driven puzzle state and environmental response

My gameplay contributions focused on the systems surrounding spellcasting: managers, interactables, quest state, environmental reactions, and player feedback.

Problem

Spells needed to do more than play an effect; they had to change puzzle state and communicate progression clearly in VR.

Solution

I built and integrated C# managers and interactable systems that listened for spell-driven events and updated quest/world state.

Implementation

Unity, C#, ScriptableObjects, event-driven communication, XR interactions, Animator/VFX/SFX integration, playtesting.

Result / impact: Spells became visible progression tools across three complete quest points, with world transformations and feedback tied to player actions.

Seasick Scallywags · Unity Developer / Level Designer

Chunk management, enemy spawning, and encounter pacing

An eight-week Unity project where large levels, enemy behavior, and progression had to remain stable enough to playtest and iterate quickly.

Problem

Large environments and many enemies increased rendering/gameplay cost while early layouts and encounter density created navigation and pacing issues.

Solution

I implemented distance-based chunk activation, scalable spawning, six enemy encounter types, and redesigned terrain/navigation based on testing.

Implementation

Unity, C#, terrain APIs, enemy AI, projectiles, Animator, VFX/SFX, Git, playtesting, lifecycle management.

Result / impact: Two playable levels with progressive difficulty, six encounter archetypes, and a complete build shaped through repeated testing.

VR Ocean Site One · Unity Developer / Animator

Predator behavior connected to animation and audio

The technical challenge was not just creating a shark and barracuda—it was getting those assets to respond coherently inside an existing VR mini-game.

Problem

The predator assets needed runtime state, readable movement, and audio/animation timing that supported the biodiversity mini-game.

Solution

I integrated Animator state machines, animation events, C# behavior scripts, and reusable audio managers around the predator pipeline.

Implementation

Unity, C#, Animator Controllers, animation events, XR Toolkit, Audio Mixer, Maya asset integration.

Result / impact: The predators functioned as responsive gameplay entities rather than disconnected art assets.
